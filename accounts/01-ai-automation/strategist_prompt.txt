## ANGLE QUALITY STANDARDS

Strong angle has:
- A specific, non-obvious observation as the core
- A hook that makes someone stop mid-scroll
- A point of view — not just information
- Clear fit for one primary format

Weak angle is:
- A listicle with no perspective ("5 tools for X")
- Generic advice anyone could write
- Trend-chasing without original observation
- Missing a clear "so what"
